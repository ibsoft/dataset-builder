# Third-party components

This distribution includes locally bundled minified assets from Bootstrap 5 and CodeMirror 5. Both are distributed under the MIT license. Their public project license pages should be consulted when packaging or distributing derivative releases.

Bootstrap: https://github.com/twbs/bootstrap/blob/main/LICENSE

CodeMirror 5: https://github.com/codemirror/codemirror5/blob/master/LICENSE

Font Awesome 6.5.2 icons are loaded at runtime from cdnjs and are not bundled. Font Awesome Free icons are licensed CC BY 4.0, fonts SIL OFL 1.1, and code MIT: https://fontawesome.com/license/free

DM Sans and Space Grotesk are loaded from Google Fonts rather than bundled. The system font stack is used if the service is inaccessible.

Python library licenses are those of their respective packages installed from `requirements.txt`.
